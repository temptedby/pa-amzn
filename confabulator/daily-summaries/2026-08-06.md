# 2026-08-06 — the day Sponsored Brands stopped being invisible

Morning review found three silent failures in a working engine. The afternoon found why 83% of the
ad spend had no data behind it, after I got the answer wrong twice. Nothing was deployed; one PR is
open and waiting.

## The headline

**Sponsored Brands data is reachable through the API after all.** Not through v3, which returns
COMPLETED with zero rows, and not by rebuilding campaigns, which William rejected and was right to.
Through the **legacy v2 endpoints**, which serve the legacy campaigns exactly as they should.

First thing it showed us, for 2026-08-05:

```
$10.55   10 clicks   0 orders   phone security (PHRASE)
$ 1.90    2 clicks   0 orders   phone retractable holder (PHRASE)
$ 1.38    1 click    0 orders   phone assured (PHRASE)
118 keywords reported, 6 with activity.  $15.00 spend, $0.00 sales, 0 orders.
```

One word took 70% of the day's Sponsored Brands spend and produced nothing, at two and a half times
the $4 kill line. We could not see it that morning.

## Morning review: a healthy engine doing almost nothing

Crons fired on schedule all night. What they achieved over four days: **one bid change, zero kills,
and the same impossible keyword submitted 28 times.**

- **The reintroduction job could never work.** It requested reports keyed by date window, exited
  saying "collect on a later run", and ran once a day — so the next run's key had shifted and it
  requested fresh reports instead of collecting yesterday's. Twelve rows sat `REQUESTED` since
  08-03; `ad_reintro_cohort` had 0 rows. The engine survives the same design only because it runs
  every 6 hours and shares a UTC date with itself.
- **The harvest retries a keyword Amazon always refuses.** 28 attempts since Aug 1, every one logged
  `applied=1`, and a full keyword pull finds zero copies. `applied.kill = r.ok` treats a 207 whose
  every item failed as success (`ad-engine.ts:252-254`).
- **The oscillation, caught in the act.** `phone tethered` over six days: 0.49 → 0.58 → 0.68 → 0.80
  → 0.94, then 0.81 → 0.61 → 0.46 → 0.35. Up 92%, down 63%.

Sales are not still falling: July 84 units / $995.67, August tracking **88 units/month**. Down from
April's 218, but flat since June.

## The Sponsored Brands hunt, including two wrong answers

1. **"Reporting is broken."** Wrong. Reports return COMPLETED with 0 rows, which is a valid answer.
2. **"Rebuild the campaigns."** Wrong, and William killed it: new campaigns lose history, will not
   spend the same, and there was no guarantee they'd report either.
3. **Right answer:** v3 does not support **legacy single-ad-group** Sponsored Brands campaigns, and
   both ENABLED campaigns are exactly that (`isMultiAdGroupsEnabled: false`). The five multi-ad-group
   campaigns would report fine — they are paused with nothing to report.

**How it was found:** by printing the raw campaign objects instead of the three fields I expected.
The flag had been in every response all day, discarded by my own filter.

Then: legacy campaigns, legacy API. `POST /v2/hsa/keywords/report` and `/v2/hsa/campaigns/report`
work. `/v2/hsa/campaigns` without `/report` is a 404, and writing off v2 on that basis cost most of
a day.

## Also proved, with live writes verified by reading back

- **Sponsored Brands bids ARE writable.** `PUT /sb/keywords`, `Content-Type: application/json`,
  `Accept: application/vnd.sbkeywordresponse.v3+json`, bare array, **adGroupId required**, and the
  keyword's **campaign must be ENABLED** or it returns 207 `KEYWORD_COULD_NOT_CREATE_KEYWORD` and
  silently does nothing. Read uses a different Accept than write; mixing them up looked like a wall.
- **Sponsored Products writes are healthy**, and none of the 1,847 floored keywords sits in an ended,
  paused or archived campaign. Live test: $0.10 → $0.25, read back, reverted, read back.
- **273 paused SB keywords sit inside ENABLED campaigns**, so they are actionable by API today.

## Built (PR #1, not merged)

- reintroduction cron `30 15 * * *` → `30 */6 * * *`, the change that makes deferred collection
  possible at all. Proven by two live runs: run 1 requested, run 2 collected and promoted 10.
- ranking on **lifetime** evidence from `kw_lifetime` rather than Amazon's 95-day window
- an evidence bar of 2+ lifetime orders, after the first preview led with a word at 79.8x built on
  $0.25 and a single order, ahead of one at 24.4x on 126 orders
- ranking by lifetime **sales** rather than ratio; one copy of a duplicated word per run
- 10 per run / 40 a day, so all 176 qualifying words are live in ~4.5 days
- a $25/day circuit breaker on the reintroduced words
- a **14-day shield from the automatic bid cut**, because attribution lag makes a fresh word read as
  zero-orders. The $4 kill is not suppressed
- the bid ladder finally wired: enter $0.25, +$0.10 per idle day, ceiling $0.85, then escalate
- `src/lib/amazon/sb-v2.ts` — legacy SB reporting, with big-id precision handled
- `scripts/ad-overspend.mjs`, `scripts/spend-snapshot.mjs`, `scripts/word-spend.mjs`,
  `scripts/sb-candidates.mjs` — all read-only

188 tests, tsc clean.
