## 4. THE FINDING: 141 proven winners are switched off, and the reset has never reset anything

`ad_engine_log` holds **zero** rows with `action = 'reactivate'`, ever, going back to 2026-06-18.
The 1st-of-month reactivation did not act on 1 July and did not act on 1 August.

I dry-ran it against live Amazon rather than reasoning about it. It works. Zero errors, 270 seconds,
and it would re-enable **141 keywords**:

```
retractable phone tether   EXACT   2.41x lifetime   $1,121 spent   195 orders   PAUSED
retractable phone lanyard  EXACT   3.13x lifetime     $711 spent    84 orders   PAUSED
iphone tether              EXACT   2.18x lifetime     $737 spent    88 orders   PAUSED
phone chain                PHRASE  2.07x lifetime     $841 spent   105 orders   PAUSED
```

Narrowed to words carrying real evidence, at least $20 of lifetime spend and 5 orders at 2x or
better, **83 words are switched off holding $52,215 of lifetime sales.** Another 6 are enabled but
pinned at the $0.10 floor, where they win nothing.

I cannot prove WHY the cron never fired. What is proven is narrower and enough: it has never
reactivated anything, and a dry run today finds 141 waiting.

### Three problems with it, all fixable before 1 September

1. **Two of the 141 sit at a $102.00 bid.** `phone tethers retractable` EXACT and `phone chain
   holder` EXACT. Reactivation sets `state` and never touches `bid`, so they come back at $102 a
   click against a $0.85 account ceiling. Two clicks is $204.
2. **33 of the 141 come back at the $0.10 floor.** Switching a word on without a bid it can win with
   is half a fix, and it is the same floor trap we have hit twice before.
3. **It only reads PAUSED.** `reactivationCandidates()` is fed a `stateFilter: { include: ["PAUSED"] }`
   pull. Our own brand term `phone assured phone tether` EXACT, 12.75x on 78 orders, is ARCHIVED, so
   it is not in the 141 and never will be under the current code.

### And it must not be run today

Cross-checking the 141 against `kw_kill_ledger` for month `2026-08`: it would reverse **8 of this
month's 25 kills**, including `cell phone retractable tether` EXACT which burned $18.86 this month
with nothing to show. That is exactly what William ruled out on 08-23: lifetime evidence reopens a
word on the 1st, never mid-month. The job is correct on 1 September and wrong on 24 August.
