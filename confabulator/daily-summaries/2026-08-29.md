## 2. THE FINDING: Sponsored Brands has spent $0.00 for three days

Brands is the only channel above both kill bars and the closest thing the account has to a working
business. It has stopped buying entirely.

```

```

Those zeros are real, not unread. 118 keyword rows were fetched for each day at 06:51 today and the
spend column is zero.

Strip out `phone security`, correctly paused at 0.39x after taking $49.20 in week one, and Brands is
**$58.82 returning $136.88, 2.33x**. The back half alone is 3.27x against a 2.67x break-even.

### The cause, traced to one argument

`ad-rules.ts:1330`:

```
line 1231   searchStep(bid, since, last)        Sponsored Products. has memory.
line 1330   searchStep(bid, since, undefined)   Brands and Display. no memory.
```

Passing `undefined` for `last` does three things. The six-hour cooldown never applies, so the rule
fires 24 times a day instead of 4. The turn-around branch, which reverses a cut that made things
worse, can never run. And `since` is filled with month-to-date numbers instead of since-the-last-
change, so `isSpending` at line 1113 stays true for the rest of the month after a single click.

Once `isSpending` is true, line 1116 refuses every raise, and every remaining path is down.
