## The bid rule, rebuilt on four answers

William, in his words: *"we cut until the keyword doesnt perform as well. Less impressions less
sales then we start to go the other way little by little, itll stop spending way before $.1"*, and
*"yes it climbs if not spending or converting you raise the bid to find the optimal"*.

```
  spent $4 unprofitably this month  ->  PAUSE for the month
  no impressions, no clicks         ->  UP    $0.10   (stop at $0.85, ask)
  getting clicks, under 2x          ->  DOWN  $0.10
  getting clicks, 2x or better      ->  DOWN  $0.02
  the last move made it worse       ->  REVERSE, two cents at a time
```

**This reverses the 08-07 rule.** A profitable word used to be raised; it is now shaved. Both rules
are William's and they optimise different things, so the old one is kept in the code as history
rather than overwritten.

**Two cents, not five**, on his correction: *"you dont know what moves too slowly or fast we are
testing and rather be cautious to test a profitable keyword slowly then move too quick and turn off
the spending and lose market share."* He is right, and the reason is asymmetry: an over-fast cut
loses market share that is expensive to re-buy, an over-slow cut only costs time.

The turn-around needs a baseline, so `kw_bid_history` now records what each bid level produced.
Rows written before today have none, and the reversal deliberately does not fire on a baseline it
does not have.

**A live audit settled the over-ceiling worry.** Of the 109 enabled keywords bid above $0.85, only
**two** spent anything this month. The real one is `retractable phone holder belt clip` at $4.44 ->
$9.49, **2.14x, profitable**, which is exactly the case the new shave rule is for.

## Creative: what we have, and what is wrong with what is live

`drive-images.mjs` reported 98 files because it crawls four hard-coded roots that exclude
`AMZ1Step Content`, stops at depth 4, and accepts only JPEG and PNG. A proper crawl found 2,764.

**Of the 84 files in the 2023 Amazon package, 46 are the discontinued lanyard shoot.** More than
half is unusable, and it is still in A+ modules 1 and 3.

**The live A+, read through the API rather than by eye:** seven `STANDARD_HEADER_IMAGE_TEXT`
modules, every one with **no headline and no body text**. Every word is baked inside the JPEGs,
where Amazon cannot index it and a screen reader gets "Phone Tether" seven times.

**We hold Standard A+, not Premium.** All 11 documents return `contentType: EBC`, `badgeSet:
STANDARD`. So: 2 MB image cap not 5 MB, **no A+ video module** (video goes on the detail page), and
no 1464x625 Brand Story hero.

Amazon's structured Compatible Devices widget**, so Amazon itself answers "will it fit my phone".
