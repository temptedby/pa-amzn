## Three bugs, all confirmed against live data
1. **`applied=1` records intent, not outcome.** "phone assured retractable phone tether – durable clip-on leash for" logged as added 10 times across 08-01..08-03; a full pull of 3,458 keywords finds zero copies. Amazon refused it every time and we logged success, so it retries forever.
2. **859 redundant records of 3,458 (25%), across 541 duplicated text+match pairs.** "phone tether" exists 18 times.
