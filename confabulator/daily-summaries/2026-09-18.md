What improved:

```
                      Aug 1-17          Sep 1-17
earning keywords     21 @ 1.85x        37 @ 2.15x
bid moves        585 up / 110 down  869 up / 762 down    84% raises -> 53%
kills            68 @ $7.07 avg     112 @ $5.04 avg      fires 29% earlier
```

What got worse:

```
dead keywords       103 / $249.32     195 / $607.73
```

The engine now raises and lowers in near balance instead of raising four times for every cut, and
each dead word costs $5 to discover instead of $7. Then intake nearly doubled, we bought 92 more
dead words, and the savings vanished. **147 keywords entered the auction for the first time in
September** (reintroduction, the 09-01 reactivation, harvest). They spent $295.59 and returned
$199.82, 0.68x. 36% of the month's spend went on words the account had never run.

The split that explains the whole loss:

```
 37 keywords earned      $219.92 spend  ->  $473.56 sales   2.15x
195 keywords earned $0   $607.73 spend  ->  $0.00
```

The kill rule caught 111 of those 195 this month for **$564.69** and obeyed the rule on every one.
That is the $4 bar working exactly as designed, and the design costs about $560 a month. A receipt,
not a brake.
