## 3. I CALLED FOUR WORKING REPORTS BROKEN

Four attempts to pull Canadian and Mexican spend all came back "did not complete", and I reported
the international profiles as possibly having the same v3 reporting gap we hit on Sponsored Brands.

They were `PENDING`, not failed. Amazon's own queue times, measured on 2026-08-17:

```
hour UTC    n    range                 mean
00:00      14    0.4 to  4.8 min       2.6
12-13Z      3   28.2 to 31.5 min      29.9
```

I was polling at 12:41Z, the slowest window, for six to ten minutes. Given 35 minutes, **both
reports completed in 13.8 minutes.**

This is the same error that made us believe the report queue took nine minutes for two weeks in
August, until 08-17 showed it takes 31 seconds at midnight. Documented, then repeated.

**And it is now a deploy blocker.** The engine's inline wait is 90 seconds. When PR #14 ships, the
Canadian and Mexican runs will time out on their reports every single time unless `report-warm` is
extended to those profiles.
