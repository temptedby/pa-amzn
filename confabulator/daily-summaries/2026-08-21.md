# 2026-08-21 — the account cleared, three more countries went live, and the cost was wrong all along

INFORM closed with one click. Canada, Mexico and Brazil got real listings for the first time. And
late in the day the number the whole engine is built on turned out to be the raw unit cost, not what
a unit actually costs us.

---

## 1. INFORM is done

William clicked `Assign` on the Amazon.com row. Amazon's own words on Account Health:

> "Thank you for certifying that your business information is current."

The red deposit action is gone from the US home and "Investigate account health risk" is gone from
Account Health. The 2026-08-21 deactivation deadline is met.

**Why it took two weeks:** INFORM writes both causes as one sentence, *"You don't have a verified
bank account **or** didn't assign the verified bank account..."*. Verification is Amazon's verdict,
run once. Assignment is a routing choice you make per marketplace. The account was already Default
and already paying Brazil, Canada and Mexico, which is proof it had passed. Amazon.com was an empty
row. We spent 08-14 to 08-18 chasing the verification half with PNC letters and a signature card.

Navigation facts worth keeping: `/payments/deposit-methods` **404s even in US context**; the only
route is the home-page action. Amazon forces a fresh password on every visit. There is no file input
anywhere in the flow.

## 4. The fee that governs every export market

Measured from settlement events, because `getMyFeesEstimate` is wrong here and would have lost money
on every unit.

```
              units   avg price            FBA / unit           referral
US                    $  9.49              $2.52                  15.0%
Canada           57   CAD 29.28            CAD 8.21 = $5.96       15.0%
Mexico            2   MXN 742.87 = $43.80  MXN 136.84 = $8.07     10.0%
Brazil            5   BRL  92.66 = $17.85  BRL  37.00 = $7.13     15.0%
```

The estimator returned **CAD 3.79 against an actual CAD 8.21**. These are Remote Fulfilment orders
and Amazon charges the cross-border fee while the API quotes the domestic rate.

**Cross-border fulfilment is $6 to $8 a unit against $2.52 domestically**, and it is FLAT. On a
$9.49 product that fee, not the product, is the dominant cost. Mexico's referral is 10%, not 15%.

## 9. Where the money actually goes, and what does not fix it

```
  zero-sale keywords         146   $398.51   54% of spend, $0 back
  over break-even, w/ sales   16   $191.15
  at or under break-even      15   $ 39.91
```

**Lowering the $4 bar does almost nothing.** Worth about $11 a month. The overshoot is worth $76: 40
zero-sale words are past the bar and the average died at **$5.91**, not $4.

**And the bar is already harsh.** $4 buys about 6 clicks. At our 5.3% conversion rate a perfectly
good keyword shows zero sales after 6 clicks **73%** of the time.

```
   83 words in trial right now                x $4 = $332 of permitted spend
  146 words that spent with zero sales in Aug x $4 = $584
```

**The fix is fewer words in trial, not a cheaper trial.**

**The budget cap is not a lever at all**, and I was wrong to propose it. Per campaign:

```
  $645/day authorised, $30.48/day spent = 4.7% of cap
  biggest: $90/day cap, $15.43/day spend, 17% used
```

## 10. Does cutting ads cut organic? August already answered it

William's worry, and the right one. The month ran the experiment by accident.

```
               days   ad spend/day   ad sales/day   ORGANIC/day   total/day
  Aug 1-13      13         20.10          20.21         31.47       51.68
  Aug 14-19      6         70.94          32.64         30.56       63.19

  ad spend UP  3.53x        organic  0.97x, FLAT
```

Tripling the ads moved organic by minus 3%. One extra unit a day costs $51 a day to buy.

```
  at the Aug 1-13 level    ~4.7 units/day   +$1.04/day
  at the Aug 14-19 level   ~5.7 units/day   -$45.09/day
```

Caveat: 6 days against 13, one month, and ad sales credit the click date while organic follows the
order date.
