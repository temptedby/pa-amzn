## 6. CORRECTIONS

**I said the Brands reactivation bar was covered by the 08-31 change. It is not.**

When I raised the reactivation bar to 3.60x on 08-31 I raised it for Sponsored Products only.
Sponsored Brands runs its own reactivation inside the same cron job, on its own constant:

```
src/lib/amazon/sb-engine.ts:336    SB_REACTIVATE_MIN_ROAS = 1.92
```

That is the un-rescaled figure. 1.92x on record is 1.07x in today's money, below the 1.5x kill bar.
On 09-01 it qualified 60 Brands words at 1.92x to 2.6x, several of which had spent $400 to $800.

**I then said none of those 60 writes landed. I cannot stand behind that.**

The job logged all 60 with `applied=0`, and that flag is set from Amazon's own per-item success
set, so my first reading was that Amazon refused every one. But one of the five words that started
spending on 09-01, `phone retractable cord` BROAD, is on the qualified list and had been dark for
at least four days. It spent $5.02 and returned $13.49. Its lifetime record is 2.08x, which is
**1.16x in today's money**, below the kill bar. It only qualified because of the 1.92x constant.

The other four spenders are not on the list, so I cannot say how many of the 60 landed. The log
stores the word and the match type but not the keyword id, and the same text exists in several
campaigns, so matching cannot resolve it. **That part is UNREAD.**

The lesson is the mirror of one we already had. We knew `applied=1` can record a write Amazon
refused. Now `applied=0` has appeared on a write that looks like it landed. The flag is evidence of
what we asked, in both directions, and never of what happened.
