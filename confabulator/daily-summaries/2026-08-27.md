## 9. New rule from William

> *"The most a bid can ever cap at is $4. We're not going above that because it means one click
> needs to convert in one click."*

Exact reasoning: $4 is the kill bar, so a bid at $4 buys a word one click before it is switched off.

Checked live. Highest bids: US $2.50, Canada CAD 0.45, Mexico MXN 14.02, Display $2.10. `BID_CAP`
is already 2.50, stricter than the rule. **Mexico's 14.02 is 70 cents, not a breach** — I nearly
reported seven false violations by comparing a bare 4 to a peso figure, which is a trap already
written down in this project. Sponsored Brands came back unreadable and is reported UNREAD rather
than as zero.

Recorded in the skill. The guard test that would fail the build on a cap above the kill bar is
**not yet written**.
