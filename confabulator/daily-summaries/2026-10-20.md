## 7. The listing problem nobody had seen

Reviews are **not** pooled, read live. Amazon's Feb-May 2026 variation split ended pooling for
children with significant functional differences.

```
B07Y5GZP1T  Single   482 reviews  3.8 stars
B097MGPCPC  2-Pack    79          3.7
B0BLLJLSDP  Pro       25          3.8
B097MK5VZ4  3-Pack    14          3.6     <- every ad pointed here
```

The variation dimension is `color_name`, rendered as the "Color:" selector. Its six values, in the
order a shopper sees them:

```
BK  |  Black  |  Phone Assured Pack of 2  |  Phone Assured Pro 2-Pack  |  Phone Assured Pro 3-Pack  |  Silver

"BK"     -> B07Y5GZP1T  the Single        "Black"  -> B097MK5VZ4  the 3-Pack
```

**The 3-Pack's own swatch says "Black" and the Single's says "BK".** No title is affected. Alphabetical
sort puts "BK" first, so it reads as the default. William approved renaming to `1-Pack, 2-Pack,
3-Pack, Pro 1-Pack, Pro 2-Pack, Pro 3-Pack`; **blocked on the SP-API rotation**, and writability of
`color_name` is unprobed. When "BK" was set is **UNREAD** -- nothing in the repo or git history, and
the listings API is down. Inference only: the Single is from 2019, the black packs 2021, and the only
two sane labels belong to the Pro packs created 10-13.

**This is the one free way to send traffic to the 3-Pack.** The Single carries 482 reviews and earns
about half our orders organically; those shoppers already land on that selector.
