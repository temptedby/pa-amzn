## 6. Six-step research: it is a pricing problem

Full document: `confabulator/research/2026-10-08-ppc-for-a-low-priced-item.md`.

Every source converges on **Max CPC = Target ACOS x Price x CVR**, with break-even ACOS equal to
contribution margin. Applied to us:

```
affordable CPC = 0.374 x $9.49 x CVR
  CVR  7.0%  (our October)              $0.248
  CVR  9.09% (our best ever)            $0.323
  CVR 11.5%  (industry average)         $0.408
  CVR 15.2%  (best in the benchmark table)  $0.539
```

Market CPC is $1.18. **No conversion rate makes a $9.49 unit advertisable.** Inverted: to break even
at the $0.96 we actually pay, each unit must contribute **$10.56**. It contributes $3.55.

ClutchLoop sells at $28.99 and moves ~2,000/month. At a 37% margin that is about $10.70 of
contribution, almost exactly what the arithmetic demands.

**We are not running the ads badly:**

```
                ours          benchmark        verdict
CTR            0.69%      SP 0.3-0.7%      top of normal
CPC            $0.96      typical $1.18    we pay LESS than average
CVR            7.0%       SP 10-18%        below the range
```

Amazon's internal term for our product category is **"CRaP", Can't Realize a Profit**, applied to
items at **$15 or less**. Three of our four ASINs were at or under that line. The documented
remedies are multipacks and raising order value, explicitly not bid tuning.

**Two things I had backwards.** The kill threshold is **15-25 clicks**, not a dollar figure; our $4
bar is 4.2 clicks at our CPC, squarely in the noise. But 20 clicks costs $19.20, which is 5.4 units
of contribution, so **CPC has to fall before the correct rule becomes affordable**. We have been
fixing the rule first for two months.

## 7. Research round two: should all ads go to the larger pack?

Net supportive, one risk.

- *"A common waste is advertising every child in the family on the same broad head term, all
  converting at wildly different rates, all charged to one budget. This pattern accounts for most of
  the loss in variation accounts."* That is a description of our setup.
- *"Concentrate rather than spread. A single well-funded ad group on your best child beats six
  starved ones."* And *"head terms go to your best-converting child, once. The family shares reviews
  anyway, so the halo does the work."*
- Conversion by price band: **12.50% under $25, 10.20% from $25-50.** Both $9.99 and $16.49 sit in
  the same band, so little CVR penalty expected.
- **The risk:** a 3-Pack on single-intent keywords is a relevance mismatch, and Amazon demotes ads
  that convert poorly, so impressions can decay. Mitigation is bundle-intent keywords ("pack of 3",
  "set of", "bulk"). Not yet added.

## 11. What I got wrong today

1. **"Spend is genuinely falling."** I read 10-07 as $26.95 from the archive; the live report says
   **$47.21**. Second time in two days I quoted the newest day. Root cause found:
   `ad_day_observation.observed_on` is a **date with no hour**, and a day's SPEND keeps rising for a
   full day, so `MAX(observed_on)` cannot tell an early read from a late one. The reliable test is
   **two consecutive observations agreeing** (10-06 read $38.42 on both the 7th and 8th).
   **This is a live defect in the BACOS governor I built yesterday**, whose window ends at
   "yesterday, the last full day", the one day guaranteed to be wrong.
2. **"670 enabled Brands keywords."** 670 is the total; **349** are enabled. Only 9 at the floor,
   median $0.25, so the ratchet had not run out of room.
3. **"sb-engine reads kw_bid_history zero times and has no cooldown."** `bid-ledger.ts` is on main
   and sb-engine calls `recordBidRun`. The real gap is narrower: `planBids(candidates)` at line 221
   takes no cooldown and the ledger is **only written, never read**.
4. **"Brands had nothing to rebid."** Brands spent $0.67 on 10-05 and $1.45 on 10-06. The cause was
   section 5's early return.
5. **"The keyword report is blind where the campaign report sees money."** Not supported. Run side by
   side on the same days, **both** returned zero rows. SB daily reports are simply flaky, which is
   why `audit-spend.mjs` retries each day up to four times and the engine, which does not, would see
   nothing even if it ran.
6. **A read-back 8 seconds after a create reported 3 ad groups dark when 5 creates had succeeded.**
   Amazon had not indexed them. Then a truncated diagnostic made me say all 3 were fixed when they
   were not. Both from trusting a read taken too soon, or read too partially.
7. **"CVR 9.09% is fine / normal."** Against an 11.5% average and a 10-18% range it is **slightly
   below**. Two memories said otherwise and are corrected.
8. **US account health, day three.** The script's own guard prints
   `WARNING: NOT on United States. Every US page below will 404 and MUST NOT be read as clean.`
   The marketplace switcher is failing, so the session stays on Canada and every US deep link 404s.
   The "Healthy" I saw was **Canada's** verdict. Still **UNREAD**, cause now identified as our own
   broken selector.
