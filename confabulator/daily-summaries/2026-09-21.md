### Is $4 even a test?

At $1.04 CPC and 5.58% CVR, $4 buys 3.85 clicks. A genuinely average keyword produces zero sales in
3.85 clicks **80% of the time**. The $4 bar already kills four out of five good words on noise. It
was never a statistical threshold. It is a spending limit.

The backtest agrees. For the 41 keywords that did earn in September, cumulative spend at the moment
their first order landed:

```

```

The median earner converted before spending anything measurable. Winners announce themselves on the
first or second click.
