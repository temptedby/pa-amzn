### OAuth debugging (lessons logged)
Three false trails, all now understood:
1. The consent flow is a **browser** flow, not an email prompt — William kept waiting for an inbox notification. Fixed by `open`-ing the URL directly on his Mac.
2. The repeated "Access blocked / Error 400" was a **truncated copy-paste of the auth URL** (the `response_type=code` got cut off), not a redirect mismatch or propagation delay. The path-less `http://localhost:53682` Desktop redirect was correct the whole time. (We almost rebuilt it as a Web client for nothing — the screenshot of the actual error page is what cracked it.)
3. The Gmail API has to be explicitly **enabled** in the Cloud project (a separate 403 `SERVICE_DISABLED` until done).

### The rejection, finally read (ticket AO-40501)
Amazon Ads API Support, 2026-04-21, auto-closed Apr 28:
> "We are unable to confirm the relationship between your email domain and company name... ensure your Amazon login or the email used to access the registration form matches your company name. Please do not use your personal email to register."

Root cause: the application presented **three names** (Douglas Dean LLC / Securisee / Phone Assured) and an email that matched none (william@besocialscene.com was in the loop). The whole address/bank-statement plan was the wrong problem.
