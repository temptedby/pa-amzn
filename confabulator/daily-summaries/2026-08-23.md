## 5. The audit William asked for

*"We need an auditing system, a second brain for you to get your data right."*

`scripts/audit-spend.mjs`. The rule is structural rather than a reminder:

- Sponsored Brands is one call per day, **retried four times**
- a day that never answers is **MISSING, not zero**
- **if any day is missing, no total is printed at all**, and it exits non-zero
- a cumulative column that lines up with the console
- `--expect=879` checks itself against William's figure and says MATCH or DOES NOT MATCH

It immediately recovered the two lost Sponsored Brands days (08-12 at $4.14, 08-21 at $1.18) and
then **refused to bless its own total** against William's $879. The gap turned out to be a clock:
his reading was taken partway through today, and $879.00 sits exactly between my close of 08-22
($872.68) and 08-23 ($885.34). Both numbers were right, measured from different moments.

It now prints DAY BY DAY and CUMULATIVE as two separate blocks and never mixes them, with a per-row
note on how settled each day's sales are.

## 6. "Sales still landing, but by how much?"

Three attempts before an honest answer.

```
v1  last snapshot of each day     -> 08-20 rose 191.5%
v2  max snapshot within each day  -> 08-20 rose 191.5%
v3  monotonicity                  -> 08-20 DISCARDED
```

The engine completes two to four runs a day and a run whose report is still PENDING writes a
partial snapshot. Max-within-a-day did not help because **both** of 08-20's pulls were short. The
fix is an invariant: a month-to-date total cannot fall, so a day whose best reading is below the
previous day's has no usable closing figure and must be thrown away.

```
days old   rise        days old   rise
       0   0.0%               5   30.5%
       1   2.3%               6   53.7%
       2   15.4%              7   53.7%
       4   6.7%               8   45.2%
                              9   18.4%
```

**One observation per age.** Enough for a direction, not for a rate, and the script says so on every
row rather than burying it.
