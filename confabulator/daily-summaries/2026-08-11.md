## The creative pipeline, rebuilt on the Social Scene method

A Social Scene agent wrote two handover docs into this repo. Its diagnosis of its own early work is
ours word for word: *"a photo with a frame and text... decoration, not composition."* Its first
logged rule, from 2026-06-05, is the one we broke on 08-10: **never just overlay text on a full-bleed
photo.**

Rebuilt accordingly:

- **One renderer**, `scripts/pacr/render.mjs`. HTML and CSS through headless Chromium, not sharp with
  hand-written SVG. Type is Avenir Next, deliberately not the Helvetica default.
- **The gate refuses** text overflow, content off-canvas, text-on-face, an output path under `/tmp`,
  and anything over the A+ 2 MB cap. Every asset writes a `.manifest.json` beside it.
- **Contact sheet before anything.** This single step found, in a video whose metadata said none of
  it: two discontinued products in a lineup card, a "STRETCHES UP TO 27 INCHES" card that is the Pro
  spec on a Black listing, and a model wearing a neck lanyard.

Saved as `confabulator/CREATIVE-BUILD-PROCESS.md`, ten steps, each tied to the failure that made it.
