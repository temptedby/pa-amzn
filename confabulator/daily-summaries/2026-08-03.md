## Listing copy, rebuilt from returns data

William redirected me off invented keywords and onto customer feedback. Pulled 180 days of FBA
returns with buyer comments: 102 returns, and **72.5% came back SELLABLE**.

NOT_AS_DESCRIBED is the second-largest reason on the flagship (18 of 66) and resolves to four
factual gaps, not defects: **length** (3 buyers), **case compatibility** (4 NOT_COMPATIBLE),
**load capacity**, and the release mechanism.

**The finding that shaped everything:** our own title says "Retractable Phone Tether" and customers
return it saying *"not strong enough to hold the phone up/retract"* and *"seems better suited for an
ID card and not a heavy cell phone"*. They are describing our word, not a defect. The keyword is
manufacturing the returns.

Product facts confirmed by William: 84 cm cord (**published as 31 in**, under-promising by 2 in so
nobody can return it as "shorter than expected"), tested with an iPhone 16 at 6.00 oz, loop knot
needs a hole in the case (cut one at the bottom edge, offset from the charging port), tested in the
US before shipping, 1 year and 2 replacement clips.

And the best detail: **the soft spring is a deliberate safety decision.** A spring strong enough to
haul a phone back up can snap back on a finger or pinch skin. That turned the weakest bullet into
the strongest one.
