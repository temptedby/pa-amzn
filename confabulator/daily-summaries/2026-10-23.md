## 4. I darkened four of five ad groups, and how

`reroute-estate-split.mjs` matched existing product ads by **ASIN or SKU**. That adopted old paused
ads sitting on dead SKUs (`34-MR1G-UASV`) instead of the two SKUs already proven live, then paused
the working 3-Pack ads beside them. My safety gate confirmed an ad for the right ASIN existed; it
never checked whether that ad could sell.

```
after the apply:  Single {NOT_BUYABLE:1, AD_STATUS_LIVE:1}   2-Pack {NOT_BUYABLE:3}
```

Repaired by `fix-notbuyable-now.mjs`, matching by SKU only. `CPH-BLCK-2` and `57-P4AJ-J4AC` were
sitting paused in the same ad groups the whole time. All five read `AD_STATUS_LIVE` afterwards.

A second ordering bug preceded it: I gated keyword creation on the product ad reading
`AD_STATUS_LIVE`, which is impossible, because keywords are what completes a campaign. The status
reports the most upstream blocker:

```
no keywords yet  -> CAMPAIGN_INCOMPLETE
complete, paused -> CAMPAIGN_PAUSED
enabled          -> AD_STATUS_LIVE / NOT_BUYABLE   <- only here is the SKU judged
```
