## Bugs found by looking rather than reading the log

- **Every "transparent" PNG this renderer has ever made was opaque.** Playwright paints a white
  backdrop unless told not to. Composited over video that hid the footage completely and put white
  type on white. Same bug ate Paul's frames on 08-11. Transparency is now opt-in and **proven** by an
  alpha assert.
- **The vertical-overflow gate refused three good cards**, treating "an ancestor hides overflow" as
  proof of clipping. Every full-bleed template wraps its photo in an overflow-hidden hero, so a 2px
  line-height rounding read as a cut word. It compares rects now, and still catches a real clip.
- **fal's balance settles a minute late**, so the first clip logged $0.0000 and actually cost $0.28.
