## 4. THE WRITE: three $102 bids moved to $0.10

```
AFTER    $0.10 on all three, still PAUSED, read back from Amazon
```

### They were never ours

No row in `kw_bid_history`, none in `kw_bid_state`, none in `kw_kill_ledger`. Amazon's own console
export from **5 August already showed all three at $102 and already paused**, so the bid predates
our engine entirely. Amazon's suggested bid for that word is $0.79 to $1.38, which makes $102 a
typo for $1.02 by whoever ran the account before.

**The mechanism that let it survive:** the engine only looks at keywords that are ENABLED and appear
in a report. A paused keyword is invisible to it. All month the engine has been correctly managing a
*different copy* of `phone tethers retractable` between $0.71 and $0.85, never once seeing the
paused twin holding $102.
