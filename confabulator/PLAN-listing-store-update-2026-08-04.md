## The finding that drives everything

Amazon SQP, Q2 2026, real search volume. Our brand share of each query:

| Search query | Volume | Our impression share | Our purchases / total | Purchase share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| phone lanyard | 640,756 | 0.014% | 2 / 15,021 | 0.013% |
| phone strap | 202,890 | 0.014% | 1 / 4,015 | 0.025% |
| cell phone lanyard | 130,315 | 0.066% | 1 / 3,375 | 0.030% |
| phone tether | 71,186 | 0.374% | 5 / 2,861 | 0.175% |
| anti theft phone strap | 48,773 | 0.188% | 0 / 2,043 | 0.000% |
| phone leash | 30,213 | 0.257% | 2 / 1,052 | 0.190% |
| **retractable phone tether** | 12,190 | 0.790% | **0 / 542** | **0.000%** |
| phone retractable tether | 3,051 | 1.841% | 1 / 137 | 0.730% |
| phone leash retractable | 477 | 3.494% | 2 / 27 | **7.407%** |
| iphone pick pocket protection | 196 | 3.995% | 2 / 6 | **33.333%** |

Two things fall out of this table.

1. **"Retractable phone tether" is our own title language. 542 people bought on that query last
   quarter and none of them bought from us.** We rank for it better than almost anything else
   (0.79% impression share) and convert zero. Meanwhile "iphone pick pocket protection" converts at
   33% and "phone leash retractable" at 7.4% — but those are 2 sales out of 6 and 2 out of 27, far
   too small to act on. IGNORE THEM. The trustworthy number is "anti theft phone strap": 48,773
   searches, 2,043 purchases last quarter, and we won ZERO on 0.19% impression share. A large,
   proven demand pool we are entirely absent from.

   This independently confirms the returns finding: 102 returns in 180 days, 72.5% came back
   SELLABLE, with buyers writing "not strong enough to hold the phone up" and "better suited for an
   ID card." The word "retractable" sets an expectation the product is deliberately built not to
   meet. It costs us the sale AND causes the return.

2. **Price is not the problem.** Median click price on "phone tether" is $12.99. Ours is $10.47. We
   are cheaper than the market and still losing. Do not discount. Raise perceived value.
