# Amazon Content Playbook — what lifts conversion (read before building)

Researched against 2026 sources. Phone Assured is at **1.79% conversion (below Amazon's 3-10% floor) and 3.8★**, so the upside from fixing content is large, not marginal. Build in order of impact-per-hour.

## What actually lifts conversion (ranked, with the lever)
1. **Reviews / rating** — biggest trust lever. 4.5★ + 50 reviews ≈ **2× conversion**. Our 3.8★ is the #1 drag. Fix: Amazon **Vine** (Brand Registry perk) + the review engine already running. Content can't fully compensate for the star rating.
2. **Main image** — the click gate (CTR in search). Must be pure white bg, product ≥85% of frame, 2000×2000px. Test composition via **Manage Your Experiments**.
3. **7+ image set** — listings with 7+ high-res images (lifestyle + infographic + scale + comparison) see **up to +35% conversion**. No eligibility gate. We have 146 images to draw from.
4. **Listing video** — video listings convert **+9-15%**; authentic/UGC-style reportedly **~+23% vs polished**. 6-45s (15-30 best), 16:9, 1920×1080. We have 4 finished ad videos + 15 raw clips.
5. **A+ Content → Brand Story → Premium A+** — Basic A+ **+8%**, Premium **+20%** (Amazon's own figures). Brand Story + 5 A+ modules unlock Premium A+.
6. **Price** — at $9.49 the lever isn't cutting price, it's justifying value (warranty, durability) via content.

## Key specs to produce against
- **Main image:** white RGB 255,255,255 · ≥85% fill · 2000×2000 · 1:1 · JPEG · no text/badges.
- **Secondary (slots 2-7):** same pixels; text/infographic/lifestyle allowed (24px+ font for mobile).
- **Listing / SB video:** 15-30s · 16:9 · 1920×1080 · MP4 · captioned for muted autoplay.
- **A+ modules:** ≤2MB · full-width banner 970×300, image+text 300×300, header 970×600.
- **Brand Story:** hero 3000×600 · Focus 362×453 · Logo+desc 315×145 (DJI aerial shots are ideal heroes).
- **Title:** ≤75 characters from July 27 2026 (ours run ~88 now — must trim).

## Slideshows
Yes, slideshow-style videos are accepted as listing video and Sponsored Brands video (feature → benefit → use case → warranty → buy). But where we have real motion (boating/active), a motion edit out-converts a slideshow — use slideshows to fill gaps.
