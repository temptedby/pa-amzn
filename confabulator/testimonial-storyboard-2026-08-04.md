# Video testimonial storyboard — built from real reviews + footage we own
2026-08-04. Source: 36 unique 4/5-star reviews pulled live from B07Y5GZP1T
(3.8 stars, 480 ratings, 58% five-star + 9% four-star = 67% positive, 98 positive reviews total).
Every quote below is verbatim from a Verified Purchase review. Nothing invented.

## COMPLIANCE — read before building

Amazon prohibits review content and star ratings in listing images and in Sponsored Brands video
creative. So:
- **ON Amazon** (images, A+, SBV): use the customers' LANGUAGE, unattributed. No quotes, no stars.
- **OFF Amazon** (site, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, email): attributed testimonials are fine and
  this is where they convert hardest. We are currently absent on all of them.

## THE FINDING: reviews confirm the search data

Amazon SQP, on the queries with enough volume to trust: "anti theft phone strap" drew 48,773
searches and 2,043 purchases last quarter and we won **zero**. "retractable phone tether", our own
title language, drew 542 purchases and we won **zero** despite holding our best impression share.
(The 33% figure on "iphone pick pocket protection" is 2 sales out of 6. Sample too small to use.)

The reviews say the same thing from the other direction. Theft is the second-largest theme and it
produces the most vivid writing in the entire dataset:

> "My phone was pickpocketed a few weeks ago out at a bar. I bought this to preve[nt]..."

> "With the increase of phone theft at raves, having an extra sense of security is nice. Used the
> telephone cable types previously but the retractable line is nice. Not too bulky, holds device
> well (don't recommend but you can throw your phone at the ground and it'll be fine)"

> "So far I haven't had the experience someone trying to take my phone, but I feel safer using it."

**Two independent datasets, same answer: lead with theft and security, not with "retractable".**

# Honest gaps
- Only 36 of the 98 positive reviews were reachable. Amazon no longer paginates
  `product-reviews?pageNumber=`; it re-serves the same top set. These 36 came from slicing by star
  rating, sort order and keyword filters. The remaining ~60 need either more slice permutations or
  a manual export from Seller Central.
- All quotes are TEXT reviews. We have no customer-filmed video, so "video testimonial" here means
  our footage plus their words on screen or read by voice-over. If we want faces, that is a
  customer outreach campaign, not an editing job.
