## William's brief, verbatim

> I've been thinking about the images as well, and I actually think they could have an even bigger
> impact than the copy.
>
> A few ideas based on the strategy:
>
> - Lead with the outcome, not the product. Show the phone being protected in a real-life situation
>   rather than just the tether itself.
> - Focus on use cases instead of features — travel, airports, cruises, concerts, hiking, dog walks,
>   commuting, shopping, etc.
> - Include a "Works With" image showing compatibility with phone cases, phone grips and PopSockets®.
> - Highlight that it's more than a phone tether by showing it securing keys, wallets and ID badges.
> - Feature the One-Year Warranty and our customer support to help build trust.
> - For the Pro, visually differentiate it with messaging around premium materials and enhanced
>   durability for larger phones and more demanding everyday use.
>
> I also think the images should answer the customer's biggest questions as they swipe through:
>
> - Why do I need this?
> - How does it work?
> - Will it work with my phone?
> - Where can I use it?
> - Why should I trust Phone Assured?
> - Why choose the Pro?
>
> I think if we can tell that story visually, alongside the updated copy, we'll have a much stronger
> listing.

## The six questions as a swipe sequence

William's second list is the more actionable of the two: it is a **narrative order**, not a set of
themes. Each slot answers one question, in the order a shopper actually asks it.

| # | Question | What the slot has to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why do I need this? | The outcome, not the product. A phone being saved, not a tether on white |
| 2 | How does it work? | The mechanism, in one look |
| 3 | Will it work with my phone? | "Works With": cases, grips, PopSockets |
| 4 | Where can I use it? | Use cases: travel, airports, cruises, concerts, hiking, dog walks, commuting, shopping |
| 5 | Why should I trust Phone Assured? | One-Year Warranty, customer support |
| 6 | Why choose the Pro? | Premium materials, durability, larger phones, harder daily use |

## Why this matters more than usual here

Two things already established make images the highest-leverage lever available:

- **Price is not the problem.** We are the cheapest of ten competitors and the worst selling
  (`pa-price-is-not-the-problem`). Discounting is ruled out. That leaves conversion rate.
- **The creative is stale.** The image stack is 2019-era and slots 5 and 7 are unused
  (`image-stack-cro-lever`). It is the un-built CVR lever.

And the root cause work points the same way: the sales collapse traced to an unstable bid loop on a
**conversion floor**, so the fix is stability plus CVR, not a higher ACOS target
(`sales-drop-root-cause`).

## Constraints that already apply

- Every asset must pass the **42-rule PACR pre-build gate**, and no asset ships without the real
  product visible (`pacr-creative-rules`).
- Cord length and load capacity are still unconfirmed for BLACK and PRO, which blocks any
  "strong enough for X" claim in slot 1 or 6.
- PopSockets is a registered trademark. Compatibility claims are fine; implying endorsement is not.
