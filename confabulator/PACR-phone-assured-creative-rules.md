# PACR — Phone Assured Creative Rules
Modelled on the DES/Social Scene BRC. **Append-only. Newest rule wins.**
v1, 2026-08-04. Grounded in: Amazon's 2026 image spec, a teardown of 10 live competitors, the
William + Megan call of 2026-08-04, 180 days of returns, and 36 verified positive reviews.

-----

## ⛔ PACR IS A PRE-BUILD GATE
Every rule below is checked BEFORE anything is rendered, never after. The 2026-08-04 failure
(twenty type-only cards on a black background, no product, no people, no reference to our own
existing ad graphics) happened because I built first and judged after. That is the BRC 93 mistake.
No gate pass, no build.

## ⛔ THE ONE-LINE TEST
Every asset must answer: **would a shopper who has never heard of us understand what this does,
and believe it will hold their phone?** If it only conveys a mood, it fails.

-----

# 1. HARD FAILS — an asset with any of these does not ship

**PACR 1.** No asset without the PRODUCT visible. Type-only cards are banned outside the pure quote
format in PACR 40. The clip is what people are judging. Show it.

**PACR 2.** Never a generated or illustrated clip. The hardware must be photographed from the real
product. Competitors fake theirs with AI and their reviews say "broke as I pulled the cord". Our
credibility is that the thing in the picture is the thing in the box.

**PACR 3.** Never show a phone being hauled UP by the cord. That is the expectation that causes our
returns. Show the catch, never the climb.

**PACR 4.** No review text or star ratings inside Amazon listing images or Sponsored Brands video.
Amazon prohibits it. Attributed quotes are OFF-Amazon only.

**PACR 5.** No warranty or guarantee wording in the TITLE. Amazon prohibits it. Warranty belongs in
images, A+ and bullets.

**PACR 6.** No stock photo whose licence we have not verified. Several LIFESTYLE files in
`PA CREATIVE MASTER 2026-08` carry stock descriptions in their metadata ("Trendy round rattan woven
bag", a Dubai rooftop line). PRODUCT files are ours. Check before use.

**PACR 7.** No fear-based creative. William, on the call: we sell peace of mind, not panic. No theft
scare tactics, no dark or gloomy treatments.

**PACR 8.** No em dashes anywhere. No AI-sounding copy.

-----

# 2. AMAZON TECHNICAL SPEC (external standard, non-negotiable)

**PACR 10.** Main image: pure white RGB 255,255,255, product fills 85%+ of frame, no text, no logos,
no watermarks, no inset images, no props. Non-compliance suppresses the listing.

**PACR 11.** Render at 2000px minimum on the long side, 3000px preferred. Zoom needs 1000px+.

**PACR 12.** Fill ALL 7 to 9 image slots. Most listings use 4 to 6. **Most of the conversion lift
comes from filling slots, not from premium photography.** We currently underuse ours.

**PACR 13.** Front-load: main, then infographic, then lifestyle in slots 1 to 3.

**PACR 14.** Design for a 200x200 thumbnail. Most Amazon traffic is mobile. If the message dies at
thumbnail size, it dies.

**PACR 15.** Titles at 75 characters maximum. Enforced since 2026-07-27, Amazon AI-rewrites
non-compliant titles. All four of ours are currently 98 to 108.

**PACR 20. THE MAIN IMAGE PATTERN.** Every single competitor main image is a SPLIT FRAME: the clip
hardware on white on one side, a real person wearing it with their phone on the other. Not one shows
hardware alone. We show hardware alone. That is why we look like a badge holder.
Note: this bends Amazon's product-only rule. We follow PACR 10 on the main image and go
hard on the split-frame treatment from slot 2 onward, where it is unambiguously allowed.

**PACR 21.** They put a MATERIAL claim in the title: "Steel Cable", "Cut-Resistant Steel Cord". It
answers the durability objection before the shopper opens the page. We claim nothing. Decide what we
can honestly say.

**PACR 22.** They lead the title with ANTI-THEFT, then material, then hardware, then a use-case list
(Travel, Hiking, Skiing, Fishing, Climbing). Amazon SQP says "anti theft phone strap" drew 48,773
searches and 2,043 purchases last quarter and we won zero of them.

**PACR 23.** They show MULTIPLE units in frame (2-pack, 4-pack) to lift perceived value at a higher
price. We sell singles at the lowest price on the page and sell least.

**PACR 24.** SUPBEE's A+ runs: brand banner over a mountaineering shot, a brand-story block with a
team photo, a worn-on-body grid, an anti-theft city panel, a cross-sell strip. **We can beat the
brand-story block outright** because we have two real founder About Us takes from 2020 and they have
a stock team photo. Ours was last updated 2023-10-03.

**PACR 25.** PRICE IS NOT OUR PROBLEM. The category leader sells at $21.99. We are the cheapest of
ten and the worst selling. Never discount. Raise perceived value. Plan a price test upward once the
creative lands.

# 4. THE FOUR STORIES WE MUST TELL
William's direction on the 2026-08-04 call. Every asset serves one of these four.

## 4a. STRENGTH — the clip attached to a phone, holding

**PACR 30.** Show the cord under LOAD, taut, with the phone's full weight on it. A slack cord reads
as a badge reel. A taut cord reads as a tether.

**PACR 31.** Show the attachment point close up. The loop through the case, the clip on the belt.
Buyers do not believe the join will hold. Show the join.

**PACR 32.** Publish the weight rating on the asset itself. `[__ oz]` stays a placeholder until the
scale figure exists. **The number is the strength claim.** Nothing communicates strength like a spec.

**PACR 33.** The honest strength story is the CATCH: phone slips, cord goes taut, phone stops short
of the floor. Sequence it in three frames. Never a fourth frame where it climbs back up.

## 4b. DISCREET — hidden, no interference with how you dress

**PACR 34.** This is our only real advantage over the category. Competitors are visibly bulky, see the
carousel. Every discreet asset must be COMPARATIVE, explicitly or implicitly: ours flat under a
shirt, theirs swinging off a belt.

**PACR 35.** Shoot or select it WORN, on a body, under or against clothing. A clip on a white
background cannot demonstrate discretion.

**PACR 36.** Fashion contexts, not gear contexts. Office, city street, restaurant, travel. The
category defaults to hiking and fishing. Owning "looks fine with what you are wearing" is open space.

**PACR 37.** Language: slim, flat, hidden, forget it is there, does not get in the way of your style.
Customer words: "Very durable. Slim design." and "not too bulky."

## 4c. WARRANTY — we stand behind it

**PACR 38.** Warranty goes BEFORE the purchase, not after. It currently only appears when something
breaks, so it rescues unhappy customers and wins no new ones.

**PACR 39.** Three concrete parts, always together: one year warranty, free replacement, plus a spare
clip in the box. Competitors offer none of this.

**PACR 40.** The trust asset may be type-led, this is the ONE exception to PACR 1, but it must carry
a product shot or the founder. Our best proof is a real review titled "Company that Stands Behind
their Product". Off-Amazon we may quote it. On-Amazon we state the promise.

## 4d. MULTIPLE USES — beyond phones

**PACR 41.** Six uses, each backed by a real review or a real customer behaviour, never invented:
keys, AirPods ("I use it with my air pods, comes in very handy"), wallet, work badge, tools
("losing my box cutter was a constant frustration"), bags and zips.

**PACR 42.** LABEL every item in a multi-use grid. Megan on the call: our old images show things
without saying what they are.

**PACR 43.** Show the object ACTUALLY CLIPPED, not laid beside the clip. A flat-lay of unconnected
objects proves nothing.

**PACR 44.** Research search volume before committing title space to a non-phone use.

-----

# 5. THE CASE PROBLEM — solved, and we never told anyone

**PACR 45.** Stop telling customers to cut a hole in their case. William uses a clear tether tab
(SMLBJUTE, bought 2025-08-10, no adhesive, fits most cases). Case fit is our second-largest fixable
return reason and four returns came back coded NOT_COMPATIBLE.

**PACR 46.** Every "how it works" asset shows the tether-tab method as primary, cutting as fallback.
Consider putting a tab in the box. That converts our top compatibility complaint into a feature.

-----

# 6. VIDEO

**PACR 50.** 9:16 first, then crop 16:9 for YouTube. Hook in the first second.

**PACR 51.** Real product in every shot where hardware is visible. See PACR 2.

**PACR 52.** AI reenactments of reviews are permitted OFF-Amazon with an on-screen disclaimer, and
only for the person and the setting, never for the product.

**PACR 53.** Do not script the Type One Diabetes review. It names a child's medical condition and
needs that customer's written permission first.

**PACR 54.** We own 21 finished videos including two cut for Amazon in 2022 and 15 vertical reels.
**Check the library before generating anything.** Sponsored Brands Video is an upload, not a project.

-----

# 7. BUILD ORDER (the gate, in sequence)

1. Pick which of the four stories the asset serves (4a, 4b, 4c or 4d).
2. Pull the SOURCE asset from `PA CREATIVE MASTER 2026-08` and confirm it is ours (PACR 6).
3. Check the existing library first, do not rebuild what exists (PACR 54).
4. Confirm the product is in frame (PACR 1) and is the real product (PACR 2).
5. Confirm no forbidden element (PACR 3, 4, 5, 7, 8).
6. Confirm the Amazon spec for its slot (PACR 10 to 15).
7. Render at 2000px+.
8. Thumbnail test at 200x200 (PACR 14).
9. Only then ship to the review folder.

-----

# 8. OPEN, BLOCKING THE WORK
1. The weight rating for BLACK and PRO. Blocks PACR 32 and every comparison asset.
2. What we can honestly claim about the cord material. Blocks PACR 21.
3. Whether we put a tether tab in the box. Blocks PACR 46.
4. Product photography of the clip WORN under clothing. We have no asset that demonstrates
   discretion, which is our single best differentiator (PACR 34, 35).

# 9. v2 — 2026-08-09. ENFORCED IN CODE.

Everything above this line was a document. From here it is also a program. The rules below, and
every rule above that can be expressed mechanically, are checked by `src/lib/creative/pacr-rules.mjs`
before anything renders. Modelled on the Social Scene BRC: a gate emits a pass token, and every
renderer imports a guard that refuses to run without one.

```
node scripts/pacr/pacr-gate.mjs --intent candidate.json     # PASS -> /tmp/pacr-pass-<sha>.token
PACR_INTENT=candidate.json node scripts/pacr/pacr-guard.mjs # refuses without that exact token
```

Changing the candidate after it passes changes the hash, so the token no longer matches. There is
no way to render something the gate has not seen.

**PACR 43.** The wristband and the necklace are discontinued (William, 2026-08-09). Only BLACK and
PRO exist. No asset, no copy and no source file from the lanyard shoot. As of today the dead product
is still in **A+ modules 1, 3 and 5**, and module 3 is entirely it.
*Note on scope:* the copy check stays literal to what William said. The bare word "lanyard" is NOT
blocked in copy, because our own listings still use it as a search term. The five Drive folders that
are factually the lanyard shoot are blocked by name instead.

**PACR 44.** No load, capacity or retraction number until it has been physically measured.
ClutchLoop publishes 10 oz, Oaridey publishes 15 oz, Rogue publishes 36 inches. We publish nothing,
and we may not answer with an invented figure. The gate unblocks this only when
`confabulator/pacr-measurements.json` exists. **This is the highest-value blocked item on the page
and it is a kitchen-scale job, not a design job.**

**PACR 45.** The only weight claim we may make is the class: *"Best used with phones 171 g (6.0 oz)
and under, about the weight of an iPhone 16."* Never *"tested with"* across a phone list. Only an
iPhone 16 in a case has actually been tested.

**PACR 46.** Video must show the product within 3 seconds. The scroll decision is made in about
1.7 seconds, and the highest-performing hook type across 34,635 analysed clips is the product or
outcome shown in the first 2.

**PACR 47.** Video must carry burned-in captions. Most Amazon and social browsing is muted.

**PACR 48.** Testimonials must be a real person's honest opinion of a product they used. The FTC
treats **AI-generated testimonials as fake reviews**. Synthetic voice cloning of a real person needs
their consent AND a disclosure the audience sees without expanding anything. A material connection,
including free product, must be disclosed. AI may **edit** our own footage, upscale it, caption it
and voice a script we wrote. AI may not invent an endorser or fabricate product footage.

**PACR 49.** Alt text is required and must be English. The live A+ alt text is **Hebrew** and the
2026 draft is **Spanish**, both Amazon auto-generated, on a US listing, for two years.

**PACR 50.** A layout may not repeat within the last 6 assets on the same surface. Build 3 to 5
variants per idea and rotate on performance, not on a calendar. Hook formats fatigue by weeks 6 to 8.

## What v2 changes about the diagnosis

Two beliefs this file was partly built on turned out to be wrong, both corrected 2026-08-09 against
live data:

- **"The creative is 2019-stale."** No. `/AMZ1Step Content` in Drive is a complete professional 2023
  package: finished BLACK and PRO stacks, the A+ source, 32 RAW files at 6720x4480, a product video
  and a real customer testimonial. The library is ~2,453 Phone Assured files, not the 98 our own
  script reports.
- **"Slots are unused."** No. Every ASIN runs MAIN + 7. Competitors run 5 to 8. We are at the median.

The problem was never how old the photography is or how many slots are filled. It is what the
assets **say**: no strength number, no compatibility slot, no head-to-head, a dead product in the
A+, and a main image carrying "BEST VALUE" that Amazon's own rules prohibit.
