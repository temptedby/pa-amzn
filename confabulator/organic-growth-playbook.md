# Phone Assured — Organic Growth Playbook (2026)

Discovery/SEO layer on top of the 4-posts/day + recap calendar in `content-calendar.md`. Phone Assured only (two-companies). Researched against 2026 sources (cited inline below); seeding-ROI figures flagged as vendor/directional.

## The 2026 truth
Discovery shifted from "who follows you / how many hashtags" to **"does your text/audio/caption match what someone is searching, and did people save/share it."** Treat every platform as a search engine. And Amazon's 2026 A10 rewards off-Amazon traffic (TikTok/IG/Google) with higher organic rank — so organic social directly feeds the listing.

## Per-platform best practice
- **Instagram:** hashtags are now just topic labels (IG killed "follow hashtag" Dec 2024). Use **3-5 specific** on Reels, 0-1 on Stories. Real lever = **keyword in the first sentence** of the caption. Reels 7-15s, hook in first 1.5-3s.
- **TikTok:** it's a search engine (~40% of Gen Z search it over Google). Keyword in **first ~50 chars of caption + on-screen text + spoken aloud** (TikTok transcribes audio & reads on-screen text). **Pin a comment** with a secondary keyword. Trending sounds early, only if they fit.
- **YouTube Shorts:** keyword title (<70 chars), 15-30s, **#Shorts** mandatory, 3-5 hashtags in the **description** (first 3 show above title). Watch-through is the #1 signal.
- **Facebook:** Reels get 2-5x feed reach; ~50% Reels / 25% image-carousel / rest community. 3-5/week. No "Comment YES / tag a friend" bait (penalized) — use open questions.
- **Pinterest (sleeper channel):** search-first, 97% unbranded searches, pins drive traffic for months, ~2x FB referral traffic, highest AOV. Perfect for a never-seen category ("boating phone tether"). Each of the 146 images → keyword-titled pin linking to the listing.

## Reach signals (design for these, in order)
DM shares > saves > shares > comments > likes. Make content people **send to a friend** ("send this to whoever drops their phone off the boat") or **save** ("5 ways to never lose your phone"). Reply to every comment in the first hour.

## Repurpose rule
One source video → 4 placements. **Always strip the TikTok watermark before IG/FB/YT** (IG downranks watermarked Reels — CapCut / Repurpose.io free tier). Per platform: IG/FB 7-30s + 3-5 niche tags + keyword-first caption; YT Short keyword title + #Shorts; Pinterest clean vertical + keyword pin title/description + listing link. Branded **#phoneassured** everywhere.

## Blogs / AI-search era
A blog still helps, but the job changed to being **cited inside AI Overviews / ChatGPT answers**: answer clear sub-questions, use lists + defined terms, support claims with data, add schema. Repurpose each existing Drive blog into a Pinterest pin cluster + IG carousel + YouTube Short script + Amazon A+ copy. Publish what only we can say (the boating wedge, the warranty rationale).

## Cadence — honest caution
Every 2026 source says **quality + watch-through beats raw volume**. Keep 4/day + recap as an *atomization ceiling*, but produce **2-3 genuinely strong source pieces/day** and let atomization fan them out. Don't manufacture weak posts to hit a number.

## Metrics to watch (only these)
1) Saves + shares per post (top reach signal). 2) Watch-through / 3s hold rate. 3) Follower+view growth per platform (TikTok vs Pinterest vs IG separately). 4) Off-Amazon clicks to the listing. 5) Amazon sessions + conversion % (the downstream goal).

## What NOT to do
Watermarked cross-posts · keyword stuffing · FB tag-bait · volume over quality · 20-30 hashtags. All accounts/gifting/metrics are Phone Assured's own (two-companies).
