# Research: the category, what wins in A+, and where the social is

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Asked by William:** *"do more research on top performing A+ content and
social media for phone safety products to get inspired"*

---

## 1. The category is doubling, and it is winner-take-most

Market data for "anti-theft phone tether", US Amazon:

```
  annual market revenue      $103K
  annual search volume       161K searches
  90-day search growth       +99.7%          <- doubling
  units sold                 6-8K/yr
  peak months                April, May, June   (2.8x between busiest and quietest)
  brands                     17, and declining
  sellers                    23
  TOP 5 BRANDS               75% of demand
  TOP 5 PRODUCTS             65% of all clicks
```

**Brand share:** ClutchLoop 23%, MoKo 22%, CACOE 15%, Takyu 11%, SHANSHUI 4%, Rogue Fishing 4%.
Those six hold 79%. **Phone Assured is not among them**, so we are somewhere in the remaining 21%
split across eleven brands, in a category where the top five take two thirds of the clicks.

Two things follow. The category is not dying, it is growing fast, so our decline is share, not
market. And a category this concentrated does not reward being slightly better; it rewards being in
the top five or not being there at all.

## 2. The price finding, with a number attached

```
  category price range        $8.07 - $29.68
  average listing price       $14.07
  quoted optimal band         $15 - $100
  Phone Assured               $9.49
```

We are **32% below the category average listing price** and losing to products at two and three
times ours. This is the hardest version yet of the standing conclusion that price is not the
problem, and it now has a market average behind it rather than a competitor screenshot.

Observed separately on the search page today, and worth stating plainly: **seven of seven
competitors in our own related-products carousel were running a strikethrough list price with a
percent-off badge**, several landing under our flat $9.49. We are simultaneously the cheapest-
positioned brand and the only one with no deal badge, which means we look dearer in the grid while
earning less per unit. That is a merchandising decision, not a creative one, and it is William's.

## 3. The review moat is the real barrier

```
  average incumbent reviews    1,442
  average incumbent rating     4.3
  Phone Assured                  480 reviews, 3.8
```

We are behind on both count and rating. No A+ module or video changes this, which is why the honest
framing of everything built today is that creative can stop us manufacturing NEW bad reviews. It
cannot lift the existing average.

## 4. What the category complains about, and what we can honestly answer

Category-wide complaint themes: **durability, adhesive failure, charging problems, weight.**
Category return rate 3.9%.

"Adhesive failure" is the interesting one. Several competitors mount with a stick-on pad that fails.
**We do not use adhesive at all** — the cord loops through the case opening and knots. That is a
real, checkable differentiator we have never once said out loud, and it sits right next to the
charging-port complaint, which our own bullet already answers.

Amazon's own Alexa panel asks the same two questions on our page and on Oaridey's: *"Is the cable
strong enough?"* and *"Does it work with phone cases?"* Those are the category's two questions.

## 5. What actually lifts conversion in A+, from published testing

- **Basic A+ lifts conversion 5-10%. Premium lifts 15-20%. Both cut returns 10-15%.** We hold
  Standard/Basic (confirmed by API on 08-10), so 5-10% and the return reduction are what is
  available to us.
- **Objection-first module ordering beats spec-first by 22-38% in A/B tests.** Lead with the
  outcome and the doubt, not the feature list.
- The recommended shape is: emotional outcome header, then a four-feature quadrant, then a
  how-to-use sequence, then brand story, and the stated principle is that buyers decide on the
  result and use the specs to justify it afterwards.

**Against that, the set built today is in the right order but is missing two modules.** We have the
outcome header (01 discreet), the quadrant (02 where), the objection answer (03 warranty) and the
how-to (04 attach). Missing: **social proof**, and an **FAQ module** that kills the last doubt.
Those are the two the research says do the most work at the bottom.

## 6. Where the social actually is: TikTok Shop, not Instagram

Every named competitor is selling on **TikTok Shop**, not merely posting to TikTok:

- **ClutchLoop** — "Phone Anti-Theft Tether with braided steel line", the 23% share leader
- **Xtendo** — positioned explicitly for **festivals and raves**
- **Arden Cove** — thicker steel cable, metal housing, **shock-absorbing spring dampener**

Two observations worth acting on. Festival and rave is a named, ownable occasion that our own
reviews already mention and our creative never does. And Arden Cove markets its spring as a
*shock-absorbing dampener* — the same soft-spring engineering we have been apologising for, sold
as a feature. Ours is the safety decision William described on 08-03: a spring strong enough to haul
a phone up can snap back on a finger. They named it. We never did.

## 7. What this changes

1. **Add a social-proof module and an FAQ module.** The research puts the largest measured lift
   there and we do not have either.
2. **Say "no adhesive".** It is true, checkable, and it answers a top-four category complaint that
   several competitors cannot answer.
3. **Sell the soft spring as a dampener**, not as a limitation. Arden Cove proves the framing works.
4. **Name the occasion.** Festivals, raves, ski lifts, travel. Xtendo owns one and we own none.
5. **Do not chase price.** The market average is $14.07 and the leaders sit above it.

## 8. The finding that reaches back into the ad account

The category's four biggest keywords, with annual volume and growth:

```
  anti theft phone tether      29K/yr   +176.7%
  phone tether anti theft      27K/yr   +267.5%
  retractable phone tether     28K/yr    +85.8%
  retractable phone lanyard    21K/yr    +59.8%
```

**Production paused `retractable phone tether` at 06:00Z this morning.** It is a 28K-a-year term
growing 86%, it carried 4 of August's 8 orders, and it is now off until 1 September. It also paused
`anti theft phone strap`, a sibling of the two fastest-growing terms in the category.

We are switching off our position in the exact terms that are doubling.
