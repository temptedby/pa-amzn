PHONE ASSURED - COMPATIBILITY COPY
Prepared 2026-08-08. Plain text, ready to paste or hand to Megan.

Rule: 170 g (6.0 oz) is the line. That is the weight of an iPhone 16.
170 g and under = BLACK.  Over 170 g = PRO.

===========================================================
1. WHAT PEOPLE ACTUALLY BUY  (context, not for the listing)
===========================================================

In the US, Apple is about 60% of the market and Samsung about 22%.
Together they are over 80% of every phone sold here.

The world's ten best selling phones of 2025 were seven Apple and three
Samsung. Ranked at the top: iPhone 16, then iPhone 16 Pro Max, then
iPhone 16 Pro. Samsung's three were Galaxy A16 5G, Galaxy A06 5G and
Galaxy S25 Ultra.

Here is the part that matters for us:

  iPhone 16          170 g   BLACK
  iPhone 16 Pro Max  221 g   PRO
  iPhone 16 Pro      199 g   PRO
  iPhone 15          171 g   BLACK
  Galaxy S25 Ultra   218 g   PRO
  Galaxy A16 5G      200 g   PRO
  Galaxy A06 5G      191 g   PRO

Of every top selling phone in the world, exactly one is light enough for
BLACK, and it is the iPhone 16 itself. Everything else people are
actually carrying is a PRO phone.

So PRO is the mass market product and BLACK is the light phone product.
The listings should be written that way round.

===========================================================
2. BLACK - COMPATIBILITY BLOCK
===========================================================

Best used with phones 170 g (6.0 oz) and under, about the weight of an
iPhone 16. Got a heavier phone? Choose the Phone Assured Pro instead.

Fits phones 170 g and under, including:

Apple: iPhone 16, iPhone 15, iPhone 12, iPhone 13 mini, iPhone 12 mini,
iPhone SE (3rd generation), iPhone 17 Air

Samsung: Galaxy S25, Galaxy S24, Galaxy S23, Galaxy S22

Google: Pixel 5, Pixel 4a, Pixel 4a 5G

Works over most cases, grips and phone rings. Not sure what your phone
weighs? Look it up on the maker's spec page and compare it to 170 g.

===========================================================
4. HOW TO DESCRIBE THE PRODUCT (please keep to this)
===========================================================

The cord retracts. The phone does not get pulled up on its own.

Correct: the tether takes the weight and the slack so you can guide your
phone back to your pocket or bag one handed, and it never hits the floor.

Wrong, do not write or show this: the phone springing back up to your
hand by itself. It does not do that, and anyone who owns one will know.

Also true and worth saying: it secures keys, wallets and ID badges too,
not just phones.

===========================================================
5. THINGS WE CANNOT SAY YET
===========================================================

No weight or strength rating. We have not confirmed the cord load
capacity for BLACK or PRO, so nothing may claim "holds X pounds" or any
drop height.

No "tested with" applied to the full model list. We have tested an
iPhone 16 in a case. The lists above are a weight class guide, which is
honest and does the same job for the shopper.

PopSockets is someone else's trademark. Saying it fits over a PopSocket
grip is fine. Anything that sounds like PopSockets endorses us is not.

Google, Apple, Samsung and OnePlus names are fine in the description and
the compatibility fields. Keep them out of the product title.
