# Phone Assured — 1-2 Month Content Calendar (IG/FB + Amazon)

Reuses the Social Scene / Social Boost posting *method* only. All content, accounts, and data are Phone Assured's own (two-companies separation). Goal: a daily drumbeat of free-traffic content that (a) builds the brand, (b) feeds Amazon (Posts + A+ + creative for ads), and (c) lifts conversion while the review engine lifts the 3.8★.

## The daily engine (the Social Scene model, applied)

**4 posts/day + 1 end-of-day recap.** Each of the 4 posts is *atomized* into a string, and the day closes with a recap.

| Slot | ~Time (CT) | Post → atomized into | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8:00a | Feed post → Reel → Story | morning reach |
| 2 | 12:00p | Feed post → Reel → Story | midday |
| 3 | 4:00p | Feed post → Reel → Story | afternoon |
| 4 | 7:00p | Feed post → Reel → Story | prime/evening |
| Recap | 9:00p | **Slideshow video** of the day's 4 → Feed + Reel + Story | recap, "in case you missed it" |

Per post: the **Feed** post is the permanent anchor, the **Reel** is the discovery/reach engine (same asset cut vertical), the **Story** keeps daily frequency + top-of-mind. The **recap** is one slideshow video stitching the day's 4 visuals (the idea you liked), reinforcing the day and giving algorithm a second swing.

That's **5 source pieces/day → ~15 placements/day** once atomized. Over 8 weeks ≈ 280 source pieces. We hit that volume by **batching** (produce a week in one sitting) and **repurposing** the existing library first.

## The 5 content pillars (rotate them)

From `content-strategy.md`, grounded in cited stats:
1. **Boating / water (FLAGSHIP wedge)** — phone overboard = gone. Boat, kayak, jet ski, paddleboard, fishing, cruise. No Amazon competitor owns this.
2. **Anti-theft** — festivals, concerts, travel, transit, crowded cities (60% of thefts are opportunistic pickpocketing).
3. **Anti-drop / active** — skiing, biking, hiking, coasters, climbing.
4. **Everyday keep-it-close** — purse, gym, parents/toddlers, nurses, accessibility.
5. **Trust / warranty (differentiator)** — 1-yr warranty + free replacement clip. No competitor offers this. Lead with it weekly.

## The 8-week (2-month) thematic arc

Seasonal hooks layered on top of the rotation (June-July = summer/water/travel peak — perfect for the boating wedge).

- **Week 1 — "Phone Overboard" (boating launch week):** establish the flagship wedge hard. Boat/lake/pool every day. Pin the best Reel.
- **Week 2 — "Don't Get Pickpocketed" (travel + festival season):** anti-theft, summer travel, concerts, the 60%/$50B stats.
- **Week 3 — "Active Summer":** biking, hiking, kayaking, coasters — anti-drop in motion.
- **Week 4 — "The Warranty Nobody Else Offers":** hammer the 1-yr warranty + free clip differentiator; comparison content vs competitors.
- **Week 5 — "Real People, Real Saves":** UGC + testimonials (the Billo testimonial), customer DMs, review highlights → social proof to fight the 3.8★.
- **Week 6 — "Parents & Pros":** everyday keep-close — toddlers, nurses, gym, accessibility audiences.
- **Week 7 — "On the Water, Part 2" (boating encore):** double down on the wedge with the new AI creatives + drone footage from Drive.
- **Week 8 — "Back-to-School / End of Summer":** students, commuting, transit anti-theft, gifting setup.

Each week: ~3 Boating slots, 1 strong Warranty push, 1+ Social-proof. Reuse winners — if a Reel pops, re-cut and re-post it the next week.

## Amazon content track (runs in parallel, same source assets)

- **A+ Content / Brand Story**: build modules from the pillars — boating hero, use-case grid, the warranty/comparison module, social-proof band. Lifts the Single's conversion (the 1.79% problem).
- **Hero / main image A/B** (#24): rotate product-on-white vs boating-lifestyle vs warranty-badge via Manage Your Experiments — the "Netflix thumbnail" test. Directly targets conversion.
- **Listing video**: a 15-30s cut of the boating + warranty story on the product page.
