# Competitors

## What they do that we do not

**They compete on a published number.** Load rating and retraction force in ounces, printed on the
image. We publish none. Several advertise steel cable against our "fiber", which reads stronger to
a shopper whatever the truth is. They also run explicit OURS-vs-THEIRS comparison images.

If asked how to compete, the answer is usually **publish a number**, not lower a price.

## ClutchLoop, the one to study

Roughly 2,000 units a month at $28.99, against our $9.49. Their own reviews hand us four
differentiators we already have and do not say. Read `confabulator/` for the teardown before
proposing positioning.

## Price is not our problem

We are the **cheapest of ten competitors and the worst selling**. That is the single most important
competitive fact in this account. Never propose a discount, a coupon, or a price cut as a way to
increase sales. It has been tested and William has ruled on it.

The corollary: our price fell from $19.95 to $9.49 and revenue per order fell with it. The gap is
margin, not demand.
